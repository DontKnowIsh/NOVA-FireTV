**NOVA 1.2.1**

**Download page**
- Trailer pop-up: the trailer sits in a framed window, sound and picture start together, and if a trailer won't play NOVA tries the title's next video
- Fixed trailers that played sound over a black picture
- New **Stop searching** button for titles that are still searching, unreleased or waiting for approval: removes them from Radarr / Sonarr and their request from Seerr (files already on the server are kept)

**Choose a copy**
- Whole-season copies listed first, with a **Whole season** tag
- New shows: NOVA waits for Sonarr to load the episodes before searching (no more false "No copies found")
- If Seerr points at a quality profile that no longer exists, NOVA uses one that does
- When Radarr / Sonarr refuse something, their reason is shown

**Everywhere**
- NOVA-style pop-up messages instead of the Fire TV's grey ones
- Download finished pop-up shows a small poster of the film or show

**Install:** download `NOVA-FireTV.apk` below. It installs over 1.2.0. `NOVA-FireTV-source.tar.gz` is the full source for this APK (GPL-2.0).

⚠️ NOVA provides no content, playlists or sources. Use it only with media you own or have the right to access. The project does not condone piracy.
