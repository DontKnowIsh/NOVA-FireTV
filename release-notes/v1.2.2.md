**NOVA 1.2.2**

**Home and libraries**
- Bigger hero banner with the backdrop picture; the description is narrower and shows more lines
- Focused row now sits at the top, so no half-cut row under the preview
- Collection pages show a faded picture behind the posters

**Trailers**
- The hero plays each title's saved trailer (from Trailarr) when one exists, otherwise a short clip
- Trailer button on film and show pages; no internet needed to play saved trailers
- YouTube player removed everywhere except the Download page

**Subtitles**
- **Find subtitles** in the player and on the item page (English; Hindi and Punjabi with "Indian films" on)
- After a subtitle downloads, focus returns to Play
- CC button only appears when a title has no subtitles yet
- Clear message if a subtitle could not be saved

**Cast and people**
- Cast pop-up opens over the page in NOVA style
- Pressing OK on a credit you don't own opens the trailer pop-up with Download

**Settings**
- Shows this Fire TV's IP address (for adb connect)

**Install:** download `NOVA-FireTV.apk` below. It installs over 1.2.1. `NOVA-FireTV-source.tar.gz` is the full source for this APK (GPL-2.0).
